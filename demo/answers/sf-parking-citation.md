# How do I appeal a parking citation in San Francisco online?

1. Open your web browser and go to the SFMTA parking tickets page: https://www.sfmta.org/pay-or-contest-parking-ticket

2. On that page click the button labeled "Pay or Contest Parking Tickets".

3. On the next page, click the link or button labeled "Contest a Citation".

4. Enter the citation number and license plate (or other requested identifying info) in the fields shown, then click "Continue".

5. When prompted, choose the type of contest you want (for example "Request Administrative Review" or "Request a Hearing") by clicking the corresponding option, then click "Continue" or "Next".

6. Fill out the online contest form:
   - Select the reason for your appeal from the provided options.
   - Type a clear statement explaining why the ticket should be cancelled.
   - Attach supporting evidence (photos, meter receipts, permits) using the "Attach file", "Upload", or "Choose File" button.

7. Review your information and then click the button labeled "Submit" or "Submit Request" to send your contest.

8. Note the confirmation number shown on the screen and/or any confirmation email. If you’re required to pay a deposit to request a hearing, follow the instructions on-screen (buttons may be labeled "Pay" or "Pay Deposit").

9. Monitor your email for the SFMTA’s decision or further hearing instructions. If you requested a hearing, follow the instructions in the notice to appear by mail, phone, in-person, or virtual hearing as specified.

If you prefer, you can also call SFMTA Parking Violations at 311 (or 415-701-2311 from outside San Francisco) for assistance. If any page or button label differs from the above, use the site search box on sfmta.org to search for "contest a citation" or "pay or contest parking ticket".
